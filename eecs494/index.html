EECS 494

Game Development Portfolio

A chronicle of game development projects created for EECS 494. Each project represents a journey through the craft of interactive experiences, built with passion and forged in code.

Projects

PROJECT I

Metroid (Unity Remaster)

A faithful recreation of the classic Metroid experience, reimagined in Unity. This project focuses on capturing the essence of exploration and tight platforming that defined a genre.

Unity 6 C# 2D Platformer

MY CONTRIBUTIONS

I mainly worked on the movement mechanics, focusing on the player experience— camera movement and achieving a satisfying jump and running jump feel. I also developed the wall jump feature, which proved to be quite the challenge to get right.

PROJECT II

Mystery Room Mania

Explore a decrepit facility with only a flashlight and your wits. A tense 3D puzzle platformer that challenges players to navigate through darkness and solve environmental puzzles.

Unity 6 C# 3D Puzzle Platformer

MY CONTRIBUTIONS

Solo development project—I designed and implemented the entire game myself, from concept to completion. This includes level design, puzzle mechanics, lighting systems, and player controls.

PROJECT III

Split Heist

A Puzzle Platformer where you play as a thief trying to steal valuable technology from a secure facility. Navigate the dangerous Aetherium laboratory and solve environmental puzzles to make your escape. Be warned, Aetherium is a shady organization and they will not take kindly to your intrusion.

Unity 6 C# 3D Modeling Game Design

MY CONTRIBUTIONS

Split Heist was the final project for 494. It was a colloborative project, built by a team of 5 developers. Split Heist took a lot of inspiration from my previous game Mystery Room Mania, and I wanted to create a game that was more polished and professional. I was primarily responsible for the game design, level design, and puzzle mechanics. This project also taught me the creative aspects of game development, working with composers and artists to create a cohesive game.
