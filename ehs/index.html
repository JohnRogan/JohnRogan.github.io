Production SaaS — Healthcare Operations

A scheduling platform built from the inside out

Equity Health Systems is a multi-tenant provider scheduling platform built for a real healthcare organization. Administrators and schedulers manage daily provider assignments across clinics via drag-and-drop, define recurring schedule cadences, track coverage and understaffing, manage PTO, assign students to preceptors, and send daily and weekly schedule emails to staff — all from one place.

What It Does

Everything a scheduling team needs

Built for the real complexity of coordinating clinical providers, support staff, and students across multiple clinic locations.

[01]

Today's Providers

Drag-and-drop daily assignment board — unscheduled providers on the left, clinic grid on the right, color-coded by provider title (MD, DO, NP, PA), with a "Who's Out" PTO section.

[02]

Weekly & Monthly Calendar

Providers as rows, days as columns, with red-border alerts for understaffed clinics and click-through to daily detail. Monthly tiles show provider counts, understaffing alerts, and clinic closure management.

[03]

Schedule Planner

Recurring cadence builder with stacking and priority overrides — define a provider's repeating schedule once and let the platform project it forward.

[04]

Coverage Insights

PTO trends, clinic coverage summaries, and coverage alerts that surface staffing gaps before they become problems.

[05]

Student Preceptorship

Per-day assignment of trainees to providers across sites, included automatically in daily schedule emails.

[06]

Email Distribution

One-click daily and weekly schedule emails to providers, support staff, and students, with per-recipient delivery status. Powered by Resend.

[07]

Settings & Admin

Clinic configuration (min/max providers, operating hours), provider, staff, and student management, team invitations with email, role management, and audit access.

[08]

Demo Mode

Unauthenticated visitors can explore the full UI seeded with mock data — zero persistence and zero backend access.

Under The Hood

Architecture highlights

A React single-page application backed entirely by Supabase — Postgres with row-level security, Auth, and Deno edge functions.

01

Multi-tenancy

Organizations as the tenant boundary. Users can belong to multiple organizations and switch between them in-session, with membership, invitations, and an active-org model enforced at the database layer.

02

Three-tier RBAC

Admin (full control), support staff scheduler (manage schedules, providers, and coverage), and viewer (read-only, linked to a specific provider for a personal-schedule view). Tabs and actions are role-gated in the UI and enforced by RLS in the database.

03

Recurring schedule engine

Per-provider recurring "cadences" (date range, days of week, location, times) that stack by priority. Recurring assignments are virtualized client-side — synthetic assignments generated on the fly per date, with database-backed exception rows for one-off deletions.

04

Undo system

Context-level undo stack backed by a database table and RPCs, with a one-hour expiry window for schedule edits.

05

Security hardening

Strict Content Security Policy, no public or anonymous data surface, RLS policy linting tooling, and server-controlled email link generation so callers can't inject URLs. Each edge function validates the caller's JWT in-code and performs role and org checks before operating with a service-role client.

06

Staff-focused by design

The platform schedules staff, not patients — it stores no patient health information, keeping it simple, lightweight, and easy to adopt.
